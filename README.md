## Scene

A prototype of the **100-years-ago Principal's Office** for our Escape Room project.

The room includes OpenXR/XR Interaction Toolkit, world-space UI, custom materials and skybox, the AION time machine, Planet–Moon object-space motion, Chronal Orb spawning/orbital physics, particle feedback, spatial audio, camera teleport, and VR controller interactions.

## Controls

### Right Controller

- **Trigger** — Spawn Chronal Orb
- **A** — Change Light
- **B** — Observation View

### Left Controller

- **X** — Play Next Spatial Sound
- **Y** — Quit
